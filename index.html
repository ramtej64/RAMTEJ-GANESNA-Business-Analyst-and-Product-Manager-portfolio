About

My work sits between the people who want something built and the people who build it. I run requirement workshops, write the BRD and FRD, break the scope into epics and user stories, and keep the backlog in an order the team can actually deliver.

I have done this on five products: a business networking platform, an NFC digital business card, a live chat SaaS, a hyperlocal marketplace and an AI-enabled school management system. I have worked in Agile Scrum and Waterfall, and I stay with a feature through UAT, defects, change requests and go-live.
